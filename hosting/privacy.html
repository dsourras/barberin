Barberin

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Barberin provides barbershop management tools and branded customer booking applications. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how it is shared, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, phone number, role, and authentication identifiers.
  • Shop details such as name, address, schedules, services, staff, and business settings.
  • Appointment details such as date, time, barber, services, customer details, and status changes.
  • Profile photos and other content uploaded by a user.
  • Push notification tokens and device information needed for notifications.
  • Subscription and purchase metadata needed to verify and manage the plan. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The full raw verification response is not stored in the app database; a protected server-side store credential may be retained to synchronize entitlement status.

How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, operate bookings and schedules, send appointment notifications and reminders, provide reports and business insights, manage subscriptions, protect the service, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Storage and Sharing

Barberin uses Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate Barberin, including Firebase, Google Play, and Apple App Store services.

Retention

We retain information while an account or shop is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Deletion requests are reviewed and processed without undue delay, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Your Deletion Rights

You can delete an authenticated account from the supported in-app account settings. If you cannot access the app, use the public web form below to request deletion of your account and associated personal data. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.
