Barberin privacy controls

Request account deletion

Use this form if you cannot access the Barberin app. We will review the request, verify ownership of the account, and delete the account and associated personal data where legally permitted. You can also use the in-app delete option when available.

Before you submit

  • Use the email address connected to your Barberin account.
  • Deleting an owner account may also remove its shop data, staff links, and appointments.
  • Some information may be retained when required by law, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.
